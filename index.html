Help

How to

  • Blocks sit on a grid; open the factory floor (F or double-click a block) to place machines and belts inside.
  • Adjacent blocks always connect. Doors are open by default; click a door to close or open it.
  • Pick an item in the palette, then click to place. Shift+click an item to put it on the hotbar.
  • Simulate, then Production: items per minute per item and machine type, plus totals.
  • Layout, port positions, rotation and machine scale not marked as verified are not confirmed and may differ in the game.
