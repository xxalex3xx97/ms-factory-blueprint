Help

Getting started

  • Blocks sit on a grid; open the factory floor (F or double-click a block) to place machines and belts inside.
  • Adjacent blocks always connect. Doors are open by default.
  • Pick an item in the palette, then click to place. Shift+click an item to put it on the hotbar.
  • Simulate, then Production: items per minute per item and machine type, plus totals.
  • Theoretical: items per minute at full speed, limited by belts, no simulation needed.
  • Layout, port positions, rotation and machine scale not marked as verified are not confirmed and may differ in the game.

        Flow & legend

        The legend lives in the Flow window: open Windows, then Flow, and press Legend. It shows the machine states (idle, working, starved, blocked), the belt colours (Flow, Blockage, full belt), the !? mark for sizes not confirmed in the game, and the Layout arrows.

        Build in game

        The Blueprint Unit mod adds a station block that builds this factory for you. Export the JSON here, paste it into the unit's window in the game, check the modification cost, and it builds the factory on your station. Needs MSCore (install with MSCoreManager).
